The Strongest Remix Artist guide

The Strongest Remix Artist is a Loopit music rhythm game by Solo with over 26,000 plays. Browser gameplay on July 25, 2026 confirmed: a title screen, an empty kit setup screen, instrument selection (4 instruments chosen via keyboard navigation), a Start Mixing button, and a rhythm grid for composing beats.

Quick answer

On the setup screen, select instruments for your kit using Tab and Enter or by tapping. Once 4 instruments are chosen, tap Start Mixing to reach the rhythm grid. Tap cells in the grid to build your beat and play it back.

How to play

  • The game opens with a title screen showing 'The Strongest Remix Artist'.
  • A setup screen follows where you configure your kit by choosing instruments.
  • The setup screen starts empty; instruments must be selected one at a time.
  • After 4 instruments are selected, the Start Mixing button becomes active.
  • Tapping Start Mixing opens the rhythm grid where you compose a beat.

Controls

  • On the setup screen, tap each instrument button to add it to your kit.
  • Alternatively, use Tab to move between instrument options and Enter or Space to select.
  • Once 4 instruments fill the kit, tap Start Mixing.
  • On the rhythm grid, tap cells in each instrument row to activate beat steps.
  • Use the playback control to hear your composition.

Strategy tips

  • Choose instruments that complement each other — a beat foundation (drums/bass) plus melodic layers works well.
  • On the rhythm grid, activate cells evenly across the timeline for a steady beat.
  • You can tap cells multiple times to toggle them on or off if you want to adjust.
  • Listen to the playback and refine your pattern before sharing.

Common mistakes

  • Clicking instrument options when accessibility overlays block click events — use Tab+Enter keyboard navigation instead.
  • Leaving the kit with fewer than 4 instruments; Start Mixing is inactive until the kit is full.
  • Tapping cells in only one row; use multiple instrument rows to create a layered rhythm.
