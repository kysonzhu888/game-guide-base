Graffiti Runner guide

Graffiti Runner is a lane-switch runner with a jump button and a short failure loop. In our verified sample, a tap run ended in a busted final score state.

Last updated July 21, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Use the side taps to switch lanes and the top tap to jump. Our run ended at BUSTED with final score 3, so the guide records the reset branch rather than a full survival route.

How to play

  • The playfield shows GRAFFITI RUNNER with SCORE at the top and a phone-like game card in the center.
  • The visible controls are tap side to switch lanes and tap top to jump.
  • The run can fall into a busted final-score state quickly when timing is off.
  • RUN AGAIN is the visible reset path after a busted state.

Controls

  • Tap the side of the game area to change lanes.
  • Tap the top of the game area to jump.
  • Tap RUN AGAIN after a busted run if you want to retry.

Strategy tips

  • Keep lane changes small and timed early; late corrections are what pushed our sample into BUSTED.
  • Use the jump only when the obstacle gap is obvious rather than as a panic button.
  • Treat the run as a short reflex test and watch the score text while you move.

Common mistakes

  • Tapping too late after an obstacle is already on top of you.
  • Mixing lane-switch taps and jump taps without a plan.
  • Assuming the run will auto-recover after a bust; it does not.
