Tag You're It guide

Tag You're It is a short chase reaction game. Our replay entered the arena and captured the SCORE and BEST timer state.

Play source game
Quick answer

Drag anywhere to move and keep distance from the tagger. Your score is survival time, so smooth movement matters more than rapid taps.

How to play

  • The arena shows SCORE and BEST timers.
  • The instruction says Drag anywhere to move.
  • The tagger chases the runner immediately.
  • Our replay captured the active arena at 0.0s with a 0.5s best.

Controls

  • Drag continuously to steer.
  • Use wide arcs instead of short jitter taps.
  • Keep your finger away from the runner so you can see the tagger.

Strategy tips

  • Move diagonally away from the tagger at the start.
  • Use the open corners to make the chaser turn.
  • Do not stop to read the timer during the run.
  • Retry and compare BEST time after each run.

Common mistakes

  • Tapping instead of dragging.
  • Running straight into a wall or corner.
  • Watching SCORE instead of the tagger.
