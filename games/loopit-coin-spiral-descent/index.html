Coin Spiral Descent guide

This Loopit playable is a neon spiral control game. The screen shows Depth 1 / 70, coins, shop, and leaderboard, while the player adjusts the spiral openings so the ball can keep moving inward.

Play source game
Quick answer

Use short left-right drags to rotate the spiral and line up the gaps. Do not hold one direction too long; the useful move is small correction, wait, then correct again.

How to play

  • Open the game and wait for the neon spiral to load.
  • The top bar shows Depth 1 / 70 and Coins 0 at the start.
  • A pink ball sits near the lower part of the spiral.
  • Drag left or right to rotate the cyan spiral walls.
  • Your goal is to line up open gaps so the ball can continue moving through the spiral instead of being blocked.
  • In our recorded sample, we verified the rotation control and depth UI but did not verify the final Depth 70 reward.

Controls

  • Use short horizontal drags on the play area.
  • Drag left to rotate one way and right to rotate back.
  • Pause after each correction so you can see where the gaps are moving.
  • Avoid long continuous swipes that spin the gaps past the ball.
  • Keep your finger away from the bottom Loopit navigation bar.

Strategy tips

  • Watch the next gap, not only the current ball position.
  • Small corrections are safer than spinning the entire maze quickly.
  • If the ball is near a wall, rotate just enough to open the closest path.
  • Use Coins and Depth as progress indicators, but do not assume the first screen is the full route.
  • Because this is a dynamic game, recording a run helps more than relying on one screenshot.

Common mistakes

  • Holding one direction until the spiral over-rotates.
  • Trying to tap the shop or leaderboard before learning the control.
  • Looking at the ball only after it is already blocked.
  • Dragging from the Loopit caption area instead of the game canvas.
  • Claiming a final reward without reaching Depth 70.

Coin Spiral Descent FAQ

How do you control Coin Spiral Descent?

Use short horizontal drags to rotate the spiral gaps around the ball.

What does Depth 1 / 70 mean?

It is the long-run progress indicator. We verified the starting depth UI but not the final Depth 70 reward.

Should I tap the ball?

No. The useful control in our test was rotating the spiral with left-right drags.

Why does the ball get stuck?

The gap is probably not aligned. Rotate slightly, pause, and watch the next opening.

Is this a screenshot-only game?

No. It is dynamic, so recording and frame review are better for learning the route.
