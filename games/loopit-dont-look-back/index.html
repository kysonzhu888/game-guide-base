Don't Look Back guide

Don't Look Back is a chase/escape game. Our replay reached the Caught failure state, so the fail route is verified.

Last updated July 2, 2026

Play source game
Quick answer

Drag to move and create distance immediately. The replay was caught, so use wider movements and avoid stopping near the pursuer.

How to play

  • The opening instruction says Drag to move. Do not look back.
  • The failure state says Caught! with Play Again.
  • Our replay captured the failure path.
  • Use the failure as a route clue: movement needs to start immediately.

Controls

  • Drag continuously to steer.
  • Use long diagonals to escape.
  • Tap Play Again after getting caught.

Strategy tips

  • Move as soon as the game loads.
  • Avoid short hesitant drags.
  • Use corners to change direction only if there is space.
  • Record a longer run to find safe zones.

Common mistakes

  • Waiting before moving.
  • Dragging too close to the pursuer.
  • Stopping after the first turn.
