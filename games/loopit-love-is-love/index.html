Love Is Love guide

Love Is Love is a Loopit physics toy. Web replay showed a rain scene, two umbrellas, and mode buttons for Rain, Kinetic, Fluid, and Spiral.

Last updated July 8, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Drag the umbrellas to catch the rain, then switch the bottom mode buttons if you want different particle behavior. This pass verified the rain scene and the four visible modes.

How to play

  • The instruction says to drag the umbrellas to catch the rain.
  • Two umbrellas are visible in the rain field.
  • Bottom controls include Rain, Kinetic, Fluid, and Spiral.
  • Rain mode was selected during the first replay.

Controls

  • Drag an umbrella under falling rain.
  • Use the mode buttons to change the particle effect.
  • Replay on mobile if desktop dragging feels imprecise.

Strategy tips

  • Start in Rain mode because it clearly shows the falling particles.
  • Move one umbrella at a time so you can see whether the catch zone follows your drag.
  • Use Kinetic, Fluid, and Spiral as variation modes until a deeper pass verifies scoring.

Common mistakes

  • Expecting a passcode or fixed level clear from a physics toy.
  • Dragging too quickly and losing track of which umbrella is active.
  • Assuming all four modes have the same behavior without testing them separately.
