Click 1159 Check Mark guide

Click 1159 Check Mark is a Loopit speed-tap challenge. App capture showed 60.00s time remaining, Clicks 0, Target 1159, and a Tap to Start button.

Play source game
Quick answer

Tap to start, then average about 19.4 taps per second for 60 seconds to reach 1159. The replay verified the start state and target, not a completed 1159-click clear.

How to play

  • The challenge starts with 60.00 seconds on the timer.
  • Clicks starts at 0.
  • The visible target is 1159 clicks.
  • Tap to Start begins the timed run.

Controls

  • Tap the center target to start.
  • Keep tapping as fast and consistently as possible after the timer begins.
  • Use the share menu to copy the source link if you want to replay from the app.

Strategy tips

  • The target requires roughly 19.4 taps per second over the full minute.
  • Use two thumbs on mobile if the game accepts multi-touch.
  • Do a short warm-up before starting because the timer begins after Tap to Start.
  • Stop if the counter does not move; desktop browser replay may not match phone multi-touch behavior.

Common mistakes

  • Starting before your fingers are positioned.
  • Pausing early because the target looks impossible.
  • Claiming a clear without screenshot proof of 1159 or the win state.
