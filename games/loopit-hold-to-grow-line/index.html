Hold to Grow Line guide

Hold to Grow Line measures how close your hold length is to the target. Our replay produced 0% and Keep Practicing, proving the result state.

Play source game
Quick answer

Hold just long enough to match the target line, then release. Our replay missed the target and got 0%, so the key is shorter controlled holds.

How to play

  • The source caption says the creator could not get 100%.
  • The result screen shows a percentage.
  • Our replay got 0% and KEEP PRACTICING.
  • NEXT SHAPE appears after the result.

Controls

  • Hold to grow the line.
  • Release when it reaches the target length.
  • Use NEXT SHAPE only after reading your result.

Strategy tips

  • Do a short calibration hold first.
  • Release earlier than instinct if the line grows fast.
  • Use the percentage result to adjust the next attempt.
  • Aim for consistency, not speed.

Common mistakes

  • Holding too long.
  • Not reading the percentage result.
  • Jumping to the next shape before learning from the miss.
