Zombie Simulator guide

Zombie Simulator is a Loopit real-time sandbox. Web replay reached an active map at 14.5 seconds with Humans 4300, Armed 268, Zombies 237, and a Tap to explode prompt.

Last updated July 2, 2026

Play source game
Quick answer

Let the simulation run, then tap to explode when the crowd balance matters. Our replay verified live counters at 14.5s: 4300 humans, 268 armed, and 237 zombies.

How to play

  • The HUD tracks time, humans, armed units, and zombies.
  • The replay reached 14.5 seconds with all three population counters active.
  • A Reset button is visible, so failed or chaotic runs can be restarted quickly.
  • Tap to explode is the most explicit player action shown in this pass.

Controls

  • Let the simulation start and watch the population counters move.
  • Tap the battlefield when the Tap to explode prompt is active.
  • Use Reset if the population outcome becomes impossible to read.

Strategy tips

  • Do not spam immediately; first observe whether zombies or armed units are gaining faster.
  • Use explosion taps when clusters form, because the map is dense and timing matters.
  • Track humans, armed units, and zombies as separate signals rather than one total population.
  • For deeper routing, record a short video and compare counter deltas after each explosion.

Common mistakes

  • Ignoring the armed-unit counter and only watching zombies.
  • Resetting before the simulation has enough time to show a trend.
  • Treating Tap to explode as a decorative prompt instead of the main intervention.
