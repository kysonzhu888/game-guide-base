Chaos Clicker Game guide

Chaos Clicker Game is a dataplex clicker. The July 13 replay raised CHAOS above the first upgrade cost, bought See the Fnord, verified Fnord Level 1 and 2 C/s, and opened both Rank and System panels.

Last updated July 13, 2026

Play source game
Quick answer

Tap the central Hypersigil until CHAOS reaches 23, then buy See the Fnord. Our replay confirmed that purchase changes Fnord Level from 0 to 1 and starts 2 C/s passive income.

How to play

  • The run starts at CHAOS: 0, 1 C/Click, and Fnord Level: 0.
  • The first upgrade, See the Fnord, costs 23 Chaos.
  • Buying it raised Fnord Level to 1, started 2 C/s, and increased the next cost to 28.
  • Rank opens a Global Leaderboard modal; System opens effect toggles and an 80% volume slider.

Controls

  • Tap the central Hypersigil to gain Chaos.
  • Tap an affordable Upgrade Dataplex row to buy it.
  • Use Rank for the live leaderboard and System for visual/audio settings.

Strategy tips

  • Build at least 23 Chaos before attempting the first purchase.
  • After buying See the Fnord, let the 2 C/s passive rate continue while you inspect menus.
  • Check Fnord Level and the next listed cost to confirm that the purchase applied.

Common mistakes

  • Trying to buy an upgrade while its cost is still unaffordable.
  • Assuming Rank contains records immediately; this replay showed a loading state.
  • Missing that System has independent flashes, scanlines, color filter, floating text, screen shake, and volume controls.
