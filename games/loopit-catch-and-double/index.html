Catch and Double guide

Catch and Double is a simple but fast reaction game. Our replay reached the active round and registered 1 captured ball, confirming the catcher movement and scoring loop.

Play source game
Quick answer

Tap START, keep the circle under the falling white balls, and make small horizontal corrections. Our replay verified the round through 1 captured ball.

How to play

  • Tap START from the title card.
  • Move the purple catcher circle under the falling white ball.
  • The counter changes to CAPTURED BALLS after the round begins.
  • Our replay verified at least one successful catch.

Controls

  • Drag or hold where you want the catcher to move.
  • Use short left-right adjustments rather than big swipes.
  • Keep your finger below the catcher so the falling ball remains visible.

Strategy tips

  • Center the catcher early, then correct after the ball lane is clear.
  • React to the ball position, not the text at the top.
  • If a ball appears near the edge, start moving early because the catcher needs travel time.
  • Record a full run if you want to compare how quickly the counter scales.

Common mistakes

  • Missing the START button and thinking the game is unresponsive.
  • Dragging too far and overshooting the falling ball.
  • Looking at the counter instead of the ball lane.
