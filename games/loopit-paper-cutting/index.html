Paper Cutting guide

Paper Cutting is one of the clearest new guides in this batch. Our replay moved from Step 1 Folding into Step 2 Cutting, so the basic route is verified.

Last updated July 2, 2026

Play source game
Quick answer

Drag along the dashed arrow to fold first, then draw on the red paper in Step 2. If the cut looks wrong, use Recut before Unfold.

How to play

  • Step 1 asks you to drag along the dashed arrow to fold.
  • After a successful fold, the game moves to Step 2: Cutting.
  • Draw cutting lines on the red paper.
  • Use Recut to retry the cutting step or Unfold to view the result.

Controls

  • Drag in the direction of the dashed arrow.
  • Draw deliberate cutting lines instead of scribbling.
  • Tap Recut if the shape is off before unfolding.

Strategy tips

  • Follow the fold arrow before thinking about the final shape.
  • Use fewer clean cuts; messy lines are harder to reason about.
  • After cutting, unfold only when the silhouette looks intentional.
  • Record both fold and cut screens if you are sharing a solution.

Common mistakes

  • Trying to cut before completing the fold.
  • Drawing too many random lines.
  • Using Unfold before checking the red paper shape.
