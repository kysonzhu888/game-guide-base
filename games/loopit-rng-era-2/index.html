LOOPIT RNG guide

Loopit RNG is a rolling loot simulator. One tap rolls the board, increments ROLLS, and logs the result in Recent Rolls; our observed outcome unlocked Common.

Last updated July 15, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Tap TAP TO ROLL; one verified roll produced Common and incremented ROLLS to 1.

How to play

  • The page starts with ROLLS: 0 and a TAP TO ROLL button.
  • A first roll showed an intermediate ROLLING state before settling.
  • The settled result added Common to Recent Rolls and showed NEW UNLOCK: Common.
  • Skip, Codes, Aura Index, and upgrades were visible but not deeply tested.

Controls

  • Tap TAP TO ROLL once and wait for the settled result.
  • Use the skip dropdown only after you understand the cutscene threshold behavior.
  • Read the Recent Rolls row to confirm the roll history.

Strategy tips

  • Capture the entry state before rolling.
  • Wait for the roll to settle so the rarity card is visible.
  • Use the Recent Rolls row to verify that the history updated.

Common mistakes

  • Stopping at the ROLLING intermediate state and calling it the result.
  • Assuming the first roll proves every biome or rarity.
  • Skipping the Recent Rolls list, which confirms the state history.
