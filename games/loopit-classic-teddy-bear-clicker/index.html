Classic Teddy Bear Clicker guide

Classic Teddy Bear Clicker is a Loopit collection clicker. Web replay tapped the bear and raised Total Clicks from 0 to 16.

Last updated July 2, 2026

Play source game
Quick answer

Tap Classic Teddy to build Total Clicks. Our replay verified 0 to 16 clicks; side buttons include Upgrade, Skins, Chars, Awaken, Craft, Eggs, Ranks, and Achv.

How to play

  • The top panel shows Total Clicks and +1 per tap.
  • The center character is Classic Teddy with an x1 multiplier.
  • Repeated taps raised Total Clicks from 0 to 16 in web replay.
  • Egg and side-menu systems are visible but not fully unlocked in this pass.

Controls

  • Tap the teddy bear to increase Total Clicks.
  • Use Upgrade after you have enough clicks or currency.
  • Check side menus such as Eggs, Ranks, and Achv after building progress.

Strategy tips

  • Start by tapping until the click counter visibly changes.
  • Track multiplier text under the bear before spending on upgrades.
  • Do not judge egg progress by the icons alone; each egg counter started at 0 in replay.
  • A deeper replay should test Upgrade and Eggs after reaching a higher click count.

Common mistakes

  • Tapping side buttons before confirming the main click loop works.
  • Assuming skins or eggs are unlocked while all visible egg counters are still 0.
  • Calling this complete before testing Upgrade, Eggs, and Ranks.
