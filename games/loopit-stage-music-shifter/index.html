Stage Music Shifter guide

Stage Music Shifter is a Loopit stage playable. Web replay reached the Select Your Angel screen with Sophia and Michael available at Level 1 and Gabriel, Raphael, Uriel, and Seraph locked behind Hope.

Play source game
Quick answer

Start with Sophia or Michael because they are available at Level 1. Gabriel, Raphael, Uriel, and Seraph were locked in replay with 5000 Hope requirements.

How to play

  • The source card says the music changes to match the stage and credits music by Patrick stoltie.
  • The replay reached a Select Your Angel screen after the opening state.
  • Sophia and Michael were visible as Level 1 available choices.
  • Gabriel, Raphael, Uriel, and Seraph appeared locked with 5000 Hope requirements.

Controls

  • Tap through the opening screen to reach character selection.
  • Choose Sophia or Michael for the first run.
  • Return later to locked angels after earning enough Hope.

Strategy tips

  • Pick an unlocked angel first instead of trying locked options repeatedly.
  • Use the 5000 Hope lock text as your medium-term unlock target.
  • Because the game emphasizes stage music, replay with audio on when comparing stages.
  • Record later runs if you want to map which stage triggers which music style.

Common mistakes

  • Trying to select locked angels before earning Hope.
  • Ignoring Sophia and Michael because the locked characters look more advanced.
  • Writing music-style conclusions without replaying the stages with audio enabled.

Creator and community notes

  • Naked Path Music is the Loopit display name shown on the source card for Stage Music Shifter.
  • The source text credits music by Patrick stoltie and asks players which style they like best.
  • Visible source-page engagement during capture: 89 views, 1 like, 1 comment, 3 remixes.
  • No separate reliable stage-by-stage music guide was confirmed during this pass. This page uses source metadata and web replay screenshots captured on July 3, 2026.
