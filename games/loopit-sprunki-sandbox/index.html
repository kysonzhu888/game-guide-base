Sprunki Sandbox guide

Sprunki Sandbox opens into a tabbed sandbox and a gallery modal. Our run verified the sandbox view and the Gallery of the Fallen & Changed overlay.

Last updated July 20, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Open the sandbox, then use the visible tabs to explore. The verified route reaches the Gallery of the Fallen & Changed overlay; deeper character behavior remains pending.

How to play

  • The sandbox view exposes tabs for Kissing Mode, Settings, Bios, Achievements, Gallery, and Sandbox.
  • The main panel prompts you to drag characters into the sandbox area.
  • Opening Gallery reveals the Gallery of the Fallen & Changed overlay.

Controls

  • Tap the visible tabs to switch between the sandbox sections.
  • Drag characters into the sandbox area when the page asks for it.
  • Tap Close to dismiss the gallery overlay.

Strategy tips

  • Read the tab labels before assuming the sandbox is a single-screen toy.
  • Use the gallery overlay as a checkpoint that the page is fully interactive.
  • Keep the sandbox and gallery states separate when describing the route.

Common mistakes

  • Treating the gallery overlay as the entire game.
  • Skipping the tab row and missing the sandbox state.
  • Claiming deeper character behavior when only the sandbox and gallery were verified.
