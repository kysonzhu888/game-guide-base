Quick answer

Start by scrolling through the six-page cover flow. The first verified page says Pool Me If You Can and Scroll for more (1/6); winner details are advertised for pages 4 and 5.

How to play

  • The first screen reads Pool Me If You Can.
  • The subtitle says Prank, Play, and Win $800+.
  • The bottom prompt says Scroll for more (1/6).
  • The source description says winners are out on pages 4 and 5.

Controls

  • Scroll inside the phone-like game panel.
  • Move page by page so you do not skip the winner announcement pages.
  • Use the source share page rather than the raw CDN iframe.

Strategy tips

  • Treat this as a multi-page announcement flow first, not an arcade game.
  • Stop on pages 4 and 5 to check the advertised winner details.
  • Capture each page if you are verifying prize or Discord instructions.

Common mistakes

  • Only reading page 1 and assuming there is no winner information.
  • Skipping directly through the scroll flow without recording page numbers.
  • Treating Discord claim text as verified by this site.
