Million Dollar Ball guide

Million Dollar Ball asks players to get to 1M dollars by drawing walls around a moving ball. Our replay verified the drawing mechanic and raised the visible CASH counter to $5, but did not reach the long 1M target.

Last updated July 2, 2026

Play source game
Quick answer

Draw short angled walls under the ball so it bounces toward cash pickups. Our replay verified the mechanic to CASH: $5; the 1M goal still requires a longer optimized run.

How to play

  • Open the shared game and wait for the ball and cash counter.
  • Draw cyan wall segments in front of the ball path.
  • Use the walls to redirect the green ball toward money pickups.
  • Our replay verified early progress to CASH: $5.

Controls

  • Drag to draw temporary wall lines.
  • Use short lines so the ball does not get trapped behind a long barrier.
  • Leave gaps for the ball to escape after each bounce.

Strategy tips

  • Start with shallow diagonal lines rather than vertical walls.
  • Place walls just ahead of the ball, not directly on top of it.
  • Use small rebounds to steer toward nearby money before trying big launches.
  • For a 1M attempt, record video and review where the ball loses momentum.

Common mistakes

  • Drawing a box that traps the ball away from cash.
  • Making lines too long and blocking the route.
  • Claiming the 1M target without a full long-run capture.
