Sprinkles Salon guide

Sprinkles Salon asks you to match a customer's requested hairstyle with Cut and Dye actions. The verified short-pink attempt completed the shift but earned one star.

Last updated July 16, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

For the verified one-star route, tap Cut once, Dye once, choose the first visibly pink swatch, and tap Done once.

How to play

  • The verified customer asked, 'Hi! Can I get short pink hair?'
  • The available styling actions include Cut, Dye, color swatches, and Done.
  • The tested route used one Cut action, one Dye action, and the first visibly pink swatch.
  • That route reached Shift Done with one star and the message 'Well... it's a unique style!'

Controls

  • Tap Cut once to shorten the customer's hair.
  • Tap Dye, then tap the first swatch that appears visibly pink.
  • Tap Done once to submit the style and reveal the shift result.

Strategy tips

  • Read the customer's request before changing the hairstyle.
  • Make one deliberate change at a time so the submitted route can be reproduced.
  • Treat the one-star result as a verified partial route, not as the optimal solution.

Common mistakes

  • Calling the tested route a perfect match when the outcome awarded only one star.
  • Tapping Cut or Dye repeatedly without checking the visible hairstyle state.
  • Submitting before confirming that both the requested length and color are visible.
