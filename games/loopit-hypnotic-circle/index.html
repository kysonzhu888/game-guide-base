HYPNOTIC CIRCLE guide

Hypnotic Circle is a drawing toy with a clear branch from the landing card to a Reverse Flow mode. Our verified browser pass covered that branch transition only.

Last updated July 21, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Tap ENTER CIRCLE to switch to Reverse Flow and Tap to draw. We verified the branch change; deeper drawing paths remain pending.

How to play

  • The landing card shows the circle artwork, title, prompt, and an ENTER CIRCLE button.
  • The follow-up state becomes Reverse Flow with a Tap to draw prompt.
  • The page behaves like a branching drawing toy rather than a level-based puzzle.
  • The verified route stops at the mode switch, not a completed drawing outcome.

Controls

  • Tap ENTER CIRCLE on the landing card.
  • In Reverse Flow, tap to draw.
  • Treat the page as a mode switcher before you try to make art.

Strategy tips

  • Use the entry screen to confirm you are on the right branch before drawing.
  • After the mode switch, test the drawing surface rather than expecting a textual win state.
  • Keep the branch change screenshot as your reference for the page’s actual start flow.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming the circle artwork itself is the only content and missing the mode switch.
  • Looking for a traditional level-clear result when the page is a drawing toy.
  • Stopping at the landing card and not entering the Reverse Flow branch.
