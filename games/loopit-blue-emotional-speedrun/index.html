Blue guide

Blue is a moody Loopit runner about carrying worries instead of fighting them. Our run verified the title card, the Begin the Walk entry, and the Level 1 state that asks you to catch a worry.

Last updated July 19, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Tap Begin the Walk, then use the right side to aim the thread while the left side moves the runner. The verified opening state is Level 1 with worries 0/6 and the Step 1 catch-a-worry prompt.

How to play

  • The game opens on a title card for Blue with the line the emotional speedrun.
  • The opening state shows Level 1 and a worry counter at 0/6.
  • The prompt says Step 1: Catch a Worry and explains the right-side aim control.
  • The page text says the goal is to heal 9 friends across 3 levels, which we did not fully play through.

Controls

  • Tap Begin the Walk to enter the run state.
  • Drag on the right side to aim the thread at a worry.
  • Use the left side to move while you line up the catch.
  • Follow the on-screen prompt once the target worry appears.

Strategy tips

  • Treat the opening screen as a setup step and do not rush past the prompt.
  • Keep the thread aim centered before releasing so you do not waste the first worry.
  • Read the worry counter and the level label together to stay oriented.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming the title card is the play state and missing the Begin the Walk button.
  • Dragging before the level prompt appears.
  • Ignoring the left/right split and trying to control everything with one side.
