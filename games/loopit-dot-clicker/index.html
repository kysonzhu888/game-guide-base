Quick answer

Tap Play Game to enter Terminal Active. The verified entry point is the terminal HUD with tutorial modules and upgrade tabs.

How to play

  • The share page shows Update v1.1, Dot Clicker, and Clicker Simulation Arc before play starts.
  • Play Game switches the page into Terminal Active.
  • The HUD exposes Gold Dot, Digital Core Score, Per Click, Passive Income, and upgrade pages.
  • The core click and upgrade branches remain pending.

Controls

  • Tap Play Game from the share card.
  • Use the in-game tabs such as Income, Style, Dots, and Frenzy once Terminal Active is visible.
  • The tutorial text points you toward the core, but deeper module actions were not fully traced in this run.

Strategy tips

  • Use the share card as the entry screenshot, then verify the Terminal Active HUD immediately after Play Game.
  • Treat the module tabs as separate branches and test one at a time.
  • Keep a screenshot of the HUD before trying canvas clicks or upgrades.

Common mistakes

  • Counting the share card as gameplay.
  • Assuming the core click branch was proven when only the terminal HUD was reached.
  • Skipping the module tabs and calling the game finished after the first transition.
