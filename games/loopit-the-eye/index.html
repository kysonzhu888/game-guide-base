The Eye guide

The Eye is a Loopit psychedelic eye toy. The intro card said Click / Tap to Enter the Void, and one click switched to the moving eye scene with drag-to-look instructions.

Last updated July 14, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Click the intro card once. The game enters the void scene, then lets you drag to look around and hold to accelerate.

How to play

  • The start screen is a title card with THE EYE and an entry prompt.
  • One click opens the animated eye scene.
  • After entering, the page says Drag to Look Around and Hold to Accelerate.
  • No other branches were uncovered in this run.

Controls

  • Click or tap the intro screen to begin.
  • Drag to pan the view once inside the eye scene.
  • Hold to accelerate if you want the more active mode.

Strategy tips

  • Use the intro click as the first checkpoint because it clearly changes the state.
  • Take a screenshot before and after entry; the contrast is strong.
  • Use short drags first so you can read the visual motion.

Common mistakes

  • Looking for buttons instead of clicking the intro art.
  • Assuming the title card is already the gameplay scene.
  • Forgetting that drag and hold are separate controls.
