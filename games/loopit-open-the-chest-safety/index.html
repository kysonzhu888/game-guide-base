Open the Chest Safety Notes guide

Open the Chest is a Loopit prank clicker. The source preview says Tap 67 times; this guide treats it as a safety-note page because public Reddit reports say the route can request photo-library permission and save prank images.

Last updated July 17, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Do not grant photo-library or camera permissions just to finish the prank. The verified preview asks for 67 taps, but this replay stopped before any permission-gated action.

How to play

  • The preview prompt says Tap 67 times.
  • A red-and-gold chest is shown on a cream background.
  • The project description says Open the chest!! What could be inside? and #FooledYou.
  • Public Reddit threads describe this as a prank route that may ask for photo permission.

Controls

  • Tap the chest only if you intentionally want to test the prank.
  • Deny photo-library or camera permission if the prompt appears.
  • Close the playable if it asks for permissions unrelated to ordinary tapping.

Strategy tips

  • Treat this as a permission-safety check, not a normal score guide.
  • Read the permission dialog before continuing in the Loopit app.
  • If unwanted images are saved, revoke Loopit photo permissions and remove the saved files from the device gallery.

Common mistakes

  • Granting photo-library access without reading why it is requested.
  • Continuing to tap after an unexpected permission prompt.
  • Letting a child complete the route unattended.
