Where Are You From guide

Where Are You From opens as a Flag Drawer mini app. Web replay tapped Start Creating and reached the Choose Your Country flag grid.

Last updated July 11, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Tap Start Creating, then pick a flag from the Choose Your Country grid. This replay verified the route from the landing screen into country selection.

How to play

  • The first screen says FLAG DRAWER and asks you to draw your country and share it with friends.
  • The main button is Start Creating.
  • After tapping Start Creating, the replay reached a Choose Your Country screen.
  • The country grid shows many flag tiles, including white, red, Afghanistan, American Samoa, and more.

Controls

  • Tap Start Creating on the first screen.
  • Scroll or tap through the flag grid to choose a country.
  • Use the selected flag as the starting point for the drawing flow.

Strategy tips

  • Choose the flag tile before trying to draw.
  • If the country is not visible, scan the grid row by row instead of tapping randomly.
  • For future replay, capture the draw canvas after selecting one specific flag.
  • This replay verified the entry route, not the finished drawing/share step.

Common mistakes

  • Trying to draw before selecting a country.
  • Missing the Start Creating button because the first screen looks like a splash page.
  • Assuming the first visible flag grid means the drawing is complete.
