Shift Weight Server guide

Shift Weight Server shows a waiter balancing stacked items while serving tables. Our replay verified the serving screen but did not increment Served past 0.

Last updated July 2, 2026

Play source game
Quick answer

Use small movement corrections and watch the stacked items before stepping. The verified replay stayed at Served: 0, so treat this as an early-route guide.

How to play

  • The HUD shows Served: 0 and Next table in: 10.
  • SHIFT WEIGHT is the main prompt.
  • STEP appears as the movement action.
  • Our replay verified the starting service state.

Controls

  • Tap or hold STEP carefully.
  • Shift weight before the stack leans too far.
  • Avoid repeated large inputs that can throw the stack off balance.

Strategy tips

  • Wait for the stack to settle before stepping.
  • Correct lean early instead of after the tower swings wide.
  • Use short inputs and observe the waiter posture.
  • Track Served count to confirm real progress.

Common mistakes

  • Spamming STEP immediately.
  • Ignoring the stack angle.
  • Calling a clear before Served increases.
