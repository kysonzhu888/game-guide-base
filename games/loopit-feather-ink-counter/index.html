Feather Ink Counter guide

Feather Ink Counter uses a small 0/10 progress counter and punishes imprecise input. Our replay reached 6/10, then later attempts with shorter strokes reset or failed to add progress, so the full 10/10 route is still precision-sensitive.

Last updated July 2, 2026

Play source game
Quick answer

Use slow controlled strokes toward the small visible target areas. Our replay verified progress up to 6/10, but not a complete 10/10 clear.

How to play

  • Start from the visible feather/drawing area.
  • Use deliberate strokes rather than broad screen swipes.
  • Watch the counter at the top; our replay moved from 0/10 to 3/10 and then 6/10.
  • If the counter drops or stops, reset and use smaller movements around the target dot.

Controls

  • Drag carefully through the visible target area.
  • Lift and restart if your stroke drifts too far from the intended mark.
  • Use short strokes near the dot once the counter reaches the middle values.

Strategy tips

  • Prioritize accuracy over speed.
  • After each increment, pause and locate the next target before moving again.
  • At 6/10 the remaining inputs appear more sensitive, so avoid sweeping across the full canvas.
  • Record a close-up replay if you are trying to document a full 10/10 route.

Common mistakes

  • Using large swipes that miss the small target zone.
  • Continuing after a reset without noticing the counter changed.
  • Writing the guide as complete when the replay only verified 6/10.
