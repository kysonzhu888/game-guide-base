Tag Grandma! guide

Tag Grandma! is a moving-target tap toy. In our sample, each tap relocated Grandma on the card while the visible score stayed at 1.

Last updated July 21, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Tap Grandma on the card; the sprite relocates after taps. In this browser sample we verified the movement branch, but the visible score did not increment beyond 1.

How to play

  • The center card shows Tag Grandma!, the score, and a small Grandma sprite.
  • The prompt says Tap Grandma to Start! and the game is tap-driven.
  • The source card uses a high-engagement social format with views, likes, comments, and remix count.
  • The verified branch is the sprite-relocation state after taps.

Controls

  • Tap the Grandma sprite inside the card.
  • Follow the new sprite position after each tap.
  • There is no keyboard control in the verified browser sample.

Strategy tips

  • Tap cleanly on the sprite rather than on the surrounding cream card.
  • Watch the sprite position after every tap because it moves.
  • Treat the score as a visible readout, but verify it in the browser because our sample kept showing 1.

Common mistakes

  • Tapping the empty card and assuming the sprite was hit.
  • Assuming the score will increment just because Grandma moved.
  • Losing track of the relocated sprite and clicking the old position again.
