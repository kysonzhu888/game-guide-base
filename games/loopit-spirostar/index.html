Spirostar guide

Spirostar presents a geometric star outline and asks players to drag to draw it. Our replay verified the prompt but did not trigger a new-shape completion state.

Play source game
Quick answer

Drag slowly along the star outline and close each line cleanly. Our replay verified the draw prompt, not a completed new-shape state.

How to play

  • The screen says SPIROSTAR and GEOMETRIC MAGIC.
  • The prompt says DRAG TO DRAW THE STAR.
  • NEW SHAPE is visible for changing the target.
  • Our replay did not capture an accepted star.

Controls

  • Drag along the visible outline.
  • Use slow strokes at corners.
  • Lift only after completing a segment.

Strategy tips

  • Trace the outline instead of drawing freehand scribbles.
  • Keep a consistent speed through each point.
  • If the line is not accepted, clear or switch shape and try again.
  • Record a close-up run for a full completion proof.

Common mistakes

  • Drawing through the center instead of the outline.
  • Moving too quickly around sharp points.
  • Assuming visible ink means the shape was accepted.
