Choose Your Egg guide

Choose Your Egg is a small Loopit branch picker. The verified pass opened a clean OREN/PINKI selection screen, then moved into an egg-hatch prompt with OREN marked active after the first pick.

Last updated July 22, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Pick OREN or PINKI first. In the verified pass, choosing OREN opened the hatch prompt, so the useful guide is about making the branch choice rather than hunting for hidden systems.

How to play

  • The landing screen shows CHOOSE YOUR EGG with OREN and PINKI cards.
  • Picking OREN opened a hatch prompt with the egg highlighted as active.
  • The direct frame is very short and does not expose a long chain of branches in this pass.
  • Treat the egg picker as a branch-select screen before any deeper content.

Controls

  • Click the OREN or PINKI egg card.
  • Tap the egg again when the hatch prompt appears.
  • Watch the highlighted/active state to confirm your choice stuck.

Strategy tips

  • Choose the egg with the vibe you want before worrying about deeper content.
  • Use the active highlight as your success check instead of guessing from the emoji alone.
  • If you need a second branch later, restart from the egg choice rather than forcing the hatch prompt.

Common mistakes

  • Stopping at the first card without confirming the active egg state.
  • Assuming the hatch prompt is a dead end when it is actually the next state.
  • Treating the screen as a long game; in the verified pass it is a short branch picker.
