Batman City Defender guide

Batman City Defender is a Loopit tap-defense playable. The verified preview says threats are descending on Gotham and Tap to throw batarangs.

Last updated July 17, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Tap Protect Gotham to start, then tap incoming threats to throw batarangs. This pass verified the start card and control prompt; threat waves remain pending because the CDN blocked the playable.

How to play

  • The title reads Batman City Defender.
  • The start card says Threats are descending on Gotham.
  • The control prompt says Tap to throw batarangs.
  • A large Protect Gotham button starts the run.

Controls

  • Tap Protect Gotham to begin.
  • Tap threats as they descend to throw batarangs.
  • Prioritize targets closest to the bottom if multiple threats are visible.

Strategy tips

  • Keep taps short and centered on the incoming target.
  • Scan top-to-bottom so you do not miss descending threats.
  • Replay in the app to confirm whether waves speed up or add new enemy types.

Common mistakes

  • Waiting for threats to reach the bottom before tapping.
  • Tapping the button area after the round starts instead of the threats.
  • Assuming wave behavior without a playable replay.
