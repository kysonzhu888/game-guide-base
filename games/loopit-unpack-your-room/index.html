Unpack Your Room guide

Unpack Your Room is a room-design playable with floor and wall presets plus a Screenshot export. The verified run customized a Living Room and produced a real PNG export.

Last updated July 16, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Choose Living Room, open Decorate, set FLOOR to Herringbone and WALL to Geometric, then use Screenshot to export the finished room.

How to play

  • The verified route started from the Living Room scene.
  • Decorate exposes separate FLOOR and WALL preset selectors.
  • The tested combination used Herringbone flooring and a Geometric wall.
  • The Screenshot control generated a 466 by 720 PNG of the customized room.

Controls

  • Use Decorate to open the floor and wall preset controls.
  • Tap a FLOOR or WALL preset to apply it to the room preview.
  • Use Screenshot to export the current room as an image.

Strategy tips

  • Choose the room type before fine-tuning its surface presets.
  • Change one surface at a time so each visual difference remains easy to verify.
  • Export only after the preview shows the intended floor and wall combination.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming the share iframe title 'Meme Game' is the editorial name of the playable.
  • Taking a browser screenshot without testing the playable's own Screenshot export.
  • Changing multiple presets without confirming which choice produced the final appearance.
