Pie Day guide

Pie Day is a drag-to-catch reaction game. The July 13 replay moved the chef under falling pies, reached Score 2, captured Game Over, and verified Play Again resets the run to Score 0.

Last updated July 13, 2026

Play source game
Quick answer

Drag the chef horizontally under falling pies. Missing enough pies ends the run; Play Again returns the score to 0.

How to play

  • The round starts at SCORE: 0 with Drag to Catch instructions.
  • Pies fall from several horizontal positions.
  • The verified run caught two pies before Game Over.
  • Play Again restarted the game at SCORE: 0.

Controls

  • Drag the chef horizontally along the bottom of the chalkboard.
  • Align the chef with the next falling pie before it reaches the floor.
  • Use Play Again on the Game Over panel to start a fresh round.

Strategy tips

  • Watch the upper half for the next spawn before making a large move.
  • Stay near the center when no pie is close so both sides remain reachable.
  • Use smooth horizontal drags; one calibrated move caught two pies in the verified run.

Common mistakes

  • Waiting until the pie reaches the bottom before moving.
  • Dragging vertically instead of keeping the chef near the floor.
  • Calling a scored state complete without also capturing Game Over and replay.
