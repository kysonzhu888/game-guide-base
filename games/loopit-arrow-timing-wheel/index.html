Arrow Timing Wheel guide

Arrow Timing Wheel is one of the stronger candidates from this batch. The instruction is explicit: tap when the arrow hits RED. Our automated test reached Level 2 and then failed, which confirms the timing window gets stricter.

Play source game
Quick answer

Wait until the white arrow points into the red half of the wheel, then tap once. Do not spam; mistimed taps can end the run.

How to play

  • Watch the rotating arrow above the colored wheel.
  • The visible instruction says to tap when the arrow hits RED.
  • Tap once when the pointer is inside the red segment.
  • Our run reached Level 2 before a mistimed tap caused Game Over.

Controls

  • Single tap to lock in the current arrow position.
  • Use visual timing, not rapid tapping.
  • Keep your tapping finger below the wheel so it does not hide the pointer.

Strategy tips

  • Focus on the pointer tip, not the center number.
  • Tap slightly after the arrow crosses into red rather than before it reaches red.
  • Expect later levels to move faster or punish mistakes more quickly.
  • If you fail at Level 2, retry and slow down your first tap rhythm.

Common mistakes

  • Tapping while the arrow is still in green.
  • Spamming multiple taps during one rotation.
  • Watching the level number instead of the arrow tip.
