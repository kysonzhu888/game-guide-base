Escape from Einstein guide

Escape from Einstein is a Loopit stealth escape game. Browser gameplay on July 25, 2026 confirmed: a title screen (побег с острова Эйнштейна — Escape from Einstein's Island), daytime gameplay with directional movement controls, and a caught-at-night failure state.

Last updated July 25, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Use the arrow or directional buttons to move your character during the day phase. Reach the escape point before nightfall to avoid being caught. Getting caught at night results in a failure screen.

How to play

  • The title screen shows the game name in Russian (побег с острова Эйнштейна) and English.
  • Gameplay has two phases: daytime (move freely) and nighttime (danger zone).
  • During the day, use directional controls to navigate your character toward the exit.
  • At night, Einstein or a guard patrols and can catch you.
  • Being caught at night ends the run with a failure state.

Controls

  • Use the on-screen arrow buttons or keyboard arrow keys to move the character.
  • Move toward the escape point during the day phase.
  • Avoid open areas at night; stay hidden or reach the exit before darkness falls.
  • If caught, look for a restart or retry button on the failure screen.

Strategy tips

  • Plan your route before moving — the day phase has a time limit before night begins.
  • Move efficiently in a direct path rather than exploring; every step counts against the timer.
  • Use visual coordinate mouse clicks on the arrow buttons if touch input is unresponsive inside the iframe.
  • The failure state shows you were caught; study where you were to improve the next run.

Common mistakes

  • Pausing too long during the day — night falls on a timer regardless of movement.
  • Moving into open sight lines at night where the patrol can see you.
  • Not finding the restart button after the failure state; it may require scrolling or tapping the screen.
