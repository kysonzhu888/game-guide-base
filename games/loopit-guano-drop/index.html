Guano Drop guide

Guano Drop is a Loopit timing-tap gag game. Replay capture reached Level 1 on a concert-stage scene with a bird and the prompt Tap to poop.

Last updated July 2, 2026

Play source game
Quick answer

Wait for the bird position, then tap when the target moment lines up. The replay verified Level 1 and the Tap to poop prompt, but not a final hit/win state.

How to play

  • The replay opened on Level 1.
  • A bird appears above a concert-stage scene.
  • The central prompt says Tap to poop.
  • The source description says the goal is getting the guano in the mouth.

Controls

  • Tap when the bird and target timing look aligned.
  • Do not tap before the prompt appears.
  • Restart the run if the target moment is missed.

Strategy tips

  • Treat it as a timing gag rather than a precision platformer.
  • Watch the bird position first, then tap once instead of spamming blindly.
  • Use screenshots or video if mapping exact hit timing later.
  • Keep the guide wording practical because the joke is intentionally crude.

Common mistakes

  • Spamming before the bird reaches the target zone.
  • Ignoring the Level 1 label and assuming later levels were checked.
  • Publishing a hit route without a captured hit or fail result.
