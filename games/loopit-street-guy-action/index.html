Street Guy Action guide

Street Guy Action exposes a small action-control panel. Our replay verified Beg, Punch, and Jump controls.

Play source game
Quick answer

Use Jump for spacing, Punch for interaction, and Beg as the special action. This replay verified the controls but not a scored ending.

How to play

  • The control set includes Beg, Punch, and Jump.
  • Arrow buttons appear for switching or movement.
  • The source caption says I am street guy.
  • Our replay verified the control panel.

Controls

  • Tap Jump to reposition.
  • Tap Punch for the main action.
  • Use Beg as the special interaction.

Strategy tips

  • Test Jump before Punch so you understand spacing.
  • Use one action at a time and watch the character animation.
  • If a target appears, move into range before punching.
  • Treat the current guide as controls-first because no clear state was captured.

Common mistakes

  • Spamming all actions at once.
  • Punching while out of range.
  • Expecting a level objective when only controls are visible.
