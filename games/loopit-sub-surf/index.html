Graffiti Runner guide

Graffiti Runner is a Loopit lane-runner playable. The verified source preview shows Score 0, Tap side to switch lanes, and Tap top to jump.

Last updated July 17, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Use the sides of the screen to change lanes and tap the top area to jump. This replay verified the opening control prompt and score HUD; the live run was blocked by the CDN challenge.

How to play

  • The preview title reads Graffiti Runner.
  • The score starts at 0.
  • The visible control prompt says Tap side to switch lanes.
  • A second prompt says Tap top to jump.

Controls

  • Tap the left or right side of the screen to change lanes.
  • Tap the upper play area to jump over hazards.
  • Keep one thumb ready for lane switching and one for jump timing on mobile.

Strategy tips

  • Treat the first seconds as a control calibration run.
  • Switch lanes early instead of waiting until an obstacle reaches the character.
  • Use short jump taps only when the game clearly asks for a top tap.
  • Replay in the Loopit app if the web iframe remains behind the CDN challenge.

Common mistakes

  • Tapping the center only and expecting lane changes.
  • Waiting too late before switching lanes.
  • Claiming a high-score route without a playable run screenshot.
