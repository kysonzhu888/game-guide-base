Pin Launch guide

This Loopit playable is a pin-launch timing game. Tap to fire pins into the blue ball, score one point per successful pin, and clear Level 1 after 6 pins.

Play source game
Quick answer

Tap when there is open space on the ball. Level 1 clears after 6 successful pins, then Level 2 starts with the same rule but tighter timing.

How to play

  • Open the game and wait for the Tap to Launch prompt.
  • The blue ball is the target, and the pink pin launches upward from the bottom.
  • Tap once to fire one pin.
  • Each pin that lands safely raises the score by 1.
  • In our recorded run, score 6 triggered the Level Clear overlay for Level 1.
  • Level 2 begins immediately after the clear screen with the same tap-to-launch control.

Controls

  • Tap the screen to launch a single pin.
  • Do not spam before checking where existing pins are attached.
  • Use the gaps between pink pins as your launch windows.
  • After Level Clear, wait for the next level to settle before tapping again.
  • The score counter in the top-left confirms each successful hit.

Strategy tips

  • Level 1 is forgiving. Tap steadily, but leave a small pause after each hit.
  • Avoid firing into a crowded side of the ball.
  • Once several pins are attached, look for the widest empty arc before tapping.
  • Score 6 is the Level 1 target verified in our run.
  • Level 2 should be played slower because pins start taking more of the safe space.

Common mistakes

  • Rapid tapping without watching the pin positions.
  • Launching into an area already filled with pins.
  • Missing the Level Clear message and continuing to tap too early.
  • Assuming every level has exactly the same spacing.
  • Watching the launch button instead of the ball.

Pin Launch FAQ

How do you clear Level 1 in Loopit Pin Launch?

Land 6 pins safely on the blue ball. Our run showed Level Clear at score 6.

What should I watch before tapping?

Watch the existing pink pins on the ball and fire into the largest open gap.

Can I spam taps?

It works briefly at the start, but it becomes risky once several pins are attached.

What changes in Level 2?

The same tap-to-launch rule continues, but spacing becomes more important.

Is Pin Launch a drag game?

No. It is a tap timing game.
