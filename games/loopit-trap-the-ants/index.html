Trap the Ants guide

Trap the Ants is one of the stronger sandbox/action entries. Our replay raised the money counter from $0 to $16, confirming real interaction.

Last updated July 13, 2026 · 2 tracked updates

Play source game
Quick answer

Use the pencil tool to draw trap lines around ant paths, then adjust with cleanup tools. Our replay verified progress from $0 to $16.

How to play

  • The HUD shows a money counter and tool buttons.
  • Visible tools include pencil, broom, and shop.
  • Drawing lines can affect ant movement.
  • Our replay raised money from $0 to $16.

Controls

  • Use the pencil to draw trap lines.
  • Use the broom to clean or reset bad lines.
  • Open shop after earning enough money.

Strategy tips

  • Draw short converging lines near ant paths.
  • Leave a small funnel instead of blocking the whole screen.
  • Watch the money counter after each trap.
  • Clean up messy lines before adding more.

Common mistakes

  • Drawing huge lines that do not guide ants.
  • Ignoring the money counter.
  • Opening shop before earning money.
