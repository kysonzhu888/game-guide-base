Target Scanner guide

Target Scanner replaces a black-loading candidate from this batch. Our replay verified Level 1, Targets 0/5, and the drag-to-scan plus tap-trigger instruction.

Play source game
Quick answer

Drag to scan the scene, then tap the trigger only when a target is centered. Our replay verified the Level 1 objective at Targets 0/5.

How to play

  • The screen shows LEVEL 1.
  • The target counter starts at TARGETS: 0/5.
  • The instruction says DRAG TO SCAN and TAP TRIGGER TO FIRE.
  • Our replay verified the target scanner start state.

Controls

  • Drag to scan around the scene.
  • Tap the trigger when a target is lined up.
  • Use slow scan movement so targets do not pass by.

Strategy tips

  • Sweep horizontally first, then vertically.
  • Do not fire until the target is centered.
  • Track the 0/5 counter after every shot.
  • A full clear needs evidence of the counter reaching 5/5.

Common mistakes

  • Firing before scanning.
  • Dragging too quickly to see targets.
  • Calling the level clear while the counter is 0/5.
