Touch Me as Quick as Possible guide

Touch Me as Quick as Possible is a Loopit tap toy. A single click on the cat changed the page to 1 CPS, confirming that the visible counter responds immediately to touch.

Last updated July 14, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Tap the cat itself, not the surrounding frame. One successful tap is enough to flip the page into the 1 CPS state, and repeated taps should keep pushing the counter.

How to play

  • The landing screen shows a cat, a TAP FAST!! prompt, and a minimal black stage.
  • A successful tap changes the page to a 1 CPS readout.
  • No separate win screen or stage list was observed.
  • The game is effectively a fast touch counter with a cat target.

Controls

  • Click or tap the cat image.
  • Repeat taps quickly if you want to raise the CPS reading.
  • Reload the page to return to the starting state.

Strategy tips

  • Target the cat directly so the tap is unambiguous.
  • Keep the gesture simple; the game reacts to touch frequency rather than route choice.
  • Capture a before/after pair because the visible difference is small but real.

Common mistakes

  • Calling the landing screen complete without tapping.
  • Tapping the background instead of the cat.
  • Expecting campaign-style branches when the toy is a single-screen counter.
