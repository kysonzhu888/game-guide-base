Paws On Loopit guide

Paws On Loopit is a Loopit pet-gallery playable. Replay capture showed the campaign has ended, voting is closed, and browsing/search still displays pet cards.

Last updated July 12, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Voting is closed, so use it as a browse-and-search pet gallery now. The replay verified Campaign ended, 5/5 left, search by pet name or ID, and bottom tabs for Browse, Ranking, My pets, and Rules.

How to play

  • The top banner says Paws On Loopit with Cats & Dogs framing.
  • Replay capture showed Campaign ended and Voting is now closed.
  • The search box asks for pet name or ID.
  • The Browse tab still displays public pet cards even after the campaign ended.

Controls

  • Use the search field to find a pet by name or ID.
  • Switch between Browse, Ranking, My pets, and Rules from the bottom tabs.
  • Use the grid/list toggle near the search box to change browsing layout when available.

Strategy tips

  • Do not expect votes to count after the campaign has ended.
  • Start in Browse to inspect pet cards, then use Ranking if you want leaderboard context.
  • Use exact pet IDs when available because names may be duplicated.
  • Treat My pets as account-dependent; it may require the Loopit app or login state.

Common mistakes

  • Trying to vote after voting is closed.
  • Confusing campaign tickets left with active voting time.
  • Searching broad names when an exact pet ID would be more reliable.
