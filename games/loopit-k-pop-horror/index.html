K-POP HORROR guide

K-POP HORROR is a Loopit survival mini-app. DEBUT switches the page from the title card to a Heartbeat/Fame HUD, so the gameplay begins only after the start button.

Last updated July 15, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Tap DEBUT to leave the title card and open the Heartbeat/Fame HUD.

How to play

  • The title screen is a single DEBUT gate.
  • After DEBUT, the HUD shows Heartbeat, Fame, and a score value.
  • The public card showed 26 views, 9 likes, and 1 comment.
  • The movement and fail/retry rules were not yet uncovered.

Controls

  • Tap DEBUT to start.
  • Treat the Heartbeat/Fame HUD as the first verified in-game state.
  • Probe movement and scoring only after the HUD appears.

Strategy tips

  • Capture the title screen before pressing DEBUT.
  • Use the HUD transition as proof that the share page is playable.
  • If later runs expose movement, keep the HUD screenshot for comparison.

Common mistakes

  • Writing the title card off as the game itself.
  • Assuming the HUD means the whole survival loop has been solved.
  • Skipping a start-state screenshot, which hides the transition.
