LoopBox guide

LoopBox is a BeepBox-style music editor. Our run verified the idle editor and the transport change from Play to Pause.

Last updated July 20, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Press Play to start the loop and watch the transport turn to Pause. The verified route stops at the editor state; deeper composition branches remain pending.

How to play

  • The idle editor shows a Play button and the top menu row.
  • Pressing Play changes the transport to Pause.
  • The layout links out to BeepBox v4.2.2.
  • The transport sits in the header and is the quickest state check.

Controls

  • Press Play to start playback.
  • Press Pause to stop the loop.
  • Use the visible menus to explore the editor after checking the transport state.

Strategy tips

  • Use the idle state to understand the panel layout before you start editing.
  • Confirm the transport changes to Pause before assuming playback actually started.
  • Treat the menu row and transport as separate controls.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming the editor is already playing when the button still says Play.
  • Confusing the transport toggle with a composition change.
  • Skipping the idle state and missing the menu layout.
