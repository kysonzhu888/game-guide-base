Cut Into Slices guide

Cut Into Slices asks you to split food into the requested number of pieces. We verified Level 1 and Level 2: a centered single cut clears Level 1, and a centered cross cut clears Level 2 with a 98% Master Chef result.

Last updated July 2, 2026

Play source game
Quick answer

Level 1: draw one clean line through the center to make 2 slices. Level 2: draw one vertical and one horizontal center cut to make 4 slices.

How to play

  • Read the level instruction before cutting; the requested slice count changes by level.
  • Level 1 says Cut into 2 slices and gives 1 cut left. Draw one straight line through the center.
  • Tap Next Level after the Master Chef result.
  • Level 2 says Cut into 4 slices and gives 2 cuts left. Use a cross pattern: one vertical center cut and one horizontal center cut.
  • After both cuts, the result modal should show accuracy and the Next Level button.

Controls

  • Swipe once to place the cut line.
  • Use a slow, straight drag across the full pizza.
  • Do not lift your finger until the line passes through both edges.

Strategy tips

  • For 2 slices, symmetry is the goal; a centered line is enough.
  • For 4 slices, make a plus sign across the food. The two cuts should cross near the middle.
  • Begin each swipe outside one edge and finish outside the opposite edge so the slice is complete.
  • Do not rush the second cut; place it perpendicular to the first one.
  • If accuracy is low, restart and make the lines straighter and closer to the center.

Common mistakes

  • Cutting only halfway through the object.
  • Using a curved line when the game asks for even slices.
  • Forgetting that Level 2 needs two cuts, not one.
  • Putting both Level 2 cuts in the same direction instead of making a cross.
