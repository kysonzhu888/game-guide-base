Ethereal Gesture Maze guide

Ethereal Gesture Maze is a Loopit maze playable. Web replay showed a grayscale maze background and a Begin button on the title overlay.

Last updated July 8, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Press Begin, then use gesture movement carefully inside the maze. This pass verified the title overlay and maze theme, not a completed route.

How to play

  • The title reads ETHEREAL.
  • The subtitle says GESTURE MAZE.
  • A Begin button sits in the center overlay.
  • The background shows a black-and-white maze path.

Controls

  • Tap Begin to start.
  • Use slow gestures once the maze route appears.
  • Avoid quick swipes until you know the collision rules.

Strategy tips

  • Study the visible maze layout before pressing Begin.
  • Make short movements at turns.
  • If you fail, replay from the title and record which corridor caused the miss.

Common mistakes

  • Rushing into the maze without reading the Begin overlay.
  • Using long swipes in a tight corridor.
  • Treating the grayscale background as decoration rather than route context.
