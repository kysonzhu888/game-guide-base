Depth Digging guide

Depth Digging has clear numeric feedback. Our replay moved from Depth 0m and $0 to Depth 1m and $10 after 10 taps.

Play source game
Quick answer

Tap steadily to dig deeper, watch Bag capacity, and pause when monsters or loot counters appear. Our verified replay reached Depth 1m and $10.

How to play

  • The HUD tracks Depth, money, Bag capacity, TAPS, MONSTERS, and LOOT DROPS.
  • Tapping increases progress.
  • Our replay moved from 0m to 1m and $10.
  • Bag stayed 0/30 during the short verification run.

Controls

  • Tap the dig area repeatedly.
  • Do not hide the HUD with your hand.
  • Pause if monsters or loot drops begin changing.

Strategy tips

  • Use a steady rhythm instead of frantic tapping.
  • Track Bag capacity so you know when to stop or sell.
  • Depth is the clearest progression signal.
  • A longer run should record when monsters first appear.

Common mistakes

  • Only looking at money and missing Bag capacity.
  • Overtapping after a monster appears.
  • Not recording the initial depth state for comparison.
