Escapa de los Backrooms: Parte 1 guide

Escapa de los Backrooms: Parte 1 is a Spanish choice-driven survival story set in Nivel 0. The verified flashlight route crossed the distorted wall and reached ESCAPE with FIN DE LA PARTE 1.

Last updated July 16, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Start the connection, investigate the wet carpet, search and keep the abandoned backpack, enter the totally dark zone, use the military flashlight, touch and push through the distorted wall, then choose Despertar.

How to play

  • The opening database screen identifies the scenario as BACKROOMS - NIVEL 0.
  • Cordura and Hidratación are visible resources, and random events can change their exact totals between attempts.
  • The verified route collects the abandoned backpack before entering the dark zone.
  • The terminal result reads ESCAPE, Has escapado... por ahora, and FIN DE LA PARTE 1.

Controls

  • Tap ESTABLECER ENLACE DE CONEXIÓN to start the expedition.
  • Choose one Spanish story-action button at each decision point.
  • Use MOCHILA to inspect collected items or REINICIAR to restart the current run.

Strategy tips

  • Investigate the wet carpet, then search the abandoned backpack and keep everything before advancing.
  • Choose the totally dark route and turn on the military flashlight instead of retreating.
  • Touch the distorted wall, push through it, and choose Despertar to finish the verified escape route.

Common mistakes

  • Entering the dark route without first collecting the military flashlight from the backpack.
  • Treating the observed Cordura and Hidratación totals as guaranteed because random events can change them.
  • Stopping at Has escapado... por ahora without confirming the FIN DE LA PARTE 1 result.
