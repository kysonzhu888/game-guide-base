Snake Evolution Feed guide

This Loopit playable asks you to draw food paths for a snake. In our run, repeated drawn strokes fed the green snake and advanced it from Evolution 1 to Evolution 2.

Play source game
Quick answer

Draw short strokes close to the snake and food trail. Keep feeding until the progress bar under Evolution 1 fills, then the snake changes into Evolution 2.

How to play

  • Start on the Evolution 1 screen with a small green snake in the center.
  • The prompt says Draw to feed the snake, so the action is drawing strokes, not tapping.
  • Draw near the snake body so the food trail appears close enough to reach.
  • Repeat short strokes from different angles rather than one long scribble far away.
  • Watch the progress bar under the Evolution label.
  • In our recorded run, the snake changed from green Evolution 1 to blue Evolution 2 after several successful feeds.

Controls

  • Drag on the playfield to draw feeding strokes.
  • Start close to the snake for better pickup.
  • Use short angled lines that cross near the snake head.
  • Do not draw only at the edge of the screen.
  • Keep drawing until the evolution bar visibly advances.

Strategy tips

  • Small strokes near the snake work better than large decorative loops.
  • If the snake rotates or moves, draw the next stroke in front of its direction.
  • Use the evolution bar as confirmation that the food is being counted.
  • Once Evolution 2 appears, the same feeding pattern continues with the new snake.
  • Avoid covering the entire screen; targeted food paths are easier to read and repeat.

Common mistakes

  • Tapping instead of drawing.
  • Drawing far away from the snake.
  • Waiting for automatic evolution without feeding.
  • Ignoring the progress bar under the Evolution label.
  • Assuming the first green form is the final stage.

Snake Evolution Feed FAQ

How do you feed the snake?

Drag to draw short food paths near the snake. The game responds to drawn strokes, not taps.

How do you reach Evolution 2?

Keep drawing near the snake until the progress bar under Evolution 1 fills. Our run changed to a blue snake at Evolution 2.

Should I draw long lines?

Short close strokes worked better than long lines far away from the snake.

What confirms that feeding is working?

The evolution progress bar moves and the snake form eventually changes.

Is this the same as the other snake speed games?

No. This one verified a visible stage change from Evolution 1 to Evolution 2.
