LinkLab Pet Choice guide

LinkLab Pet Choice is an onboarding branch inside a room-decorating shell. The July 13 replay verified the Dog and Cat selections both dismiss the adoption modal; the no-pet branch remains pending because the guest choice persisted and Chrome could not bootstrap a fresh Loopit identity.

Last updated July 13, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

The first modal offers Dog, Cat, or a no-pet skip. Dog and Cat both return to the main room shell after selection; use a fresh guest identity to compare the skip branch.

How to play

  • The adoption modal says the first companion is free.
  • Dog and Cat are separate selectable cards.
  • A third option says I don't want a pet right now.
  • The room shell also exposes Contest, Shop, furniture, Explore Rooms, Invite Friends, and Design Layout.

Controls

  • Choose Dog or Cat on the adoption modal.
  • Use the no-pet text option only in a fresh guest session if you need the skip route.
  • After selection, use the room controls to explore furniture and social branches.

Strategy tips

  • Use a fresh browser/App guest identity for each adoption branch because the selection persists.
  • Capture the modal before choosing so all three routes remain documented.
  • Treat the room shell as the branch outcome unless the selected pet is visibly rendered.

Common mistakes

  • Claiming that a pet is visibly present when the post-selection room screenshot does not show it.
  • Reloading the same guest session and expecting the adoption modal to reset.
  • Treating Chrome's Open Loopit fallback as proof that the game itself is broken.
