Nightmare Cuphead guide

Nightmare Cuphead opens as a small survival arena: drag to move, dodge the ink, and tap chests for power-ups. The July 11 live replay opened a chest to raise SOULS COLLECTED from 0 to 100, but this still behaves like a survival run rather than a short level clear.

Last updated July 11, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Drag constantly to stay alive, use the middle of the arena for room, and tap chests only when the path is safe. The current live replay changed SOULS COLLECTED from 0 to 100 by opening one chest; treat this as a survival route, not a cleared level.

How to play

  • Press FIGHT! from the title card.
  • Keep dragging the character instead of making isolated taps.
  • Watch the SOULS COLLECTED counter and the chest prompt after the arena starts.
  • Our replay reached the active SURVIVE THE ABYSS state; no fixed clear screen appeared during the run.

Controls

  • Drag to move around the arena.
  • Tap chests for power-ups when you can reach them without crossing danger.
  • Use short movement corrections so your finger does not hide the character.

Strategy tips

  • Start near the center so you can dodge in any direction.
  • Do not tunnel on chests; staying alive is the first objective.
  • Use chest openings after hazards pass, not while moving through tight gaps.
  • If you are recording a route, capture the full survival segment because the outcome depends on movement timing.

Common mistakes

  • Running straight to a chest before checking the path.
  • Stopping movement after the arena starts.
  • Calling the guide cleared when only the survival state was verified.
