Find the Hat Guy guide

Find the Hat Guy is a hidden-object screen full of small people icons. Our replay verified the target prompt but did not capture a successful snap.

Play source game
Quick answer

Scan the crowd for the single person with a hat, then tap directly on that icon. Do not tap random faces; the goal is precision.

How to play

  • The prompt says: Find the guy with the hat. Tap to snap!
  • The screen contains many small people icons.
  • The target is likely a single icon with a hat detail.
  • Our replay verified the prompt and search board.

Controls

  • Tap the suspected hat person.
  • Use slow visual scanning before tapping.
  • Avoid dragging; this is a tap-to-select challenge.

Strategy tips

  • Scan corners first, then the dense center.
  • Look for head shapes that differ from the normal icons.
  • Zoom is not exposed in the replay, so rely on careful tapping.
  • If wrong taps reset the board, pause between attempts.

Common mistakes

  • Tapping the largest cluster without checking hats.
  • Ignoring edge icons.
  • Treating all heads as identical.
