Sipscapes guide

Sipscapes is a Loopit drink recipe game by RainbowPuppy. Browser gameplay on July 25, 2026 confirmed: name and birthday entry, main drink category screen with Mocktails tab, Mojito selection, and a full Mojito recipe displayed after tapping Make Drink.

Last updated July 25, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Enter your name and birthday on the intro screen, tap Let's Go, select a drink category such as Mocktails, pick a drink like Mojito, and tap Make Drink to see the full recipe.

How to play

  • The game opens with a name input and a birthday date picker.
  • After filling in both fields, the Let's Go button activates.
  • Tapping Let's Go moves to the main drink browser.
  • The main screen has category tabs — Mocktails was confirmed during gameplay.
  • Selecting a drink opens its recipe card with a Make Drink button.

Controls

  • Tap the name field and type your name.
  • Tap the birthday field to open the date picker and set your birthday.
  • Tap Let's Go once both fields are complete.
  • Tap a category tab (e.g., Mocktails) to browse that type of drink.
  • Tap a drink name to open its recipe card.
  • Tap Make Drink to reveal the full step-by-step recipe.

Strategy tips

  • Both the name and birthday fields must be filled before Let's Go becomes active.
  • Explore all category tabs — additional categories beyond Mocktails may be present.
  • Scroll within a category if not all drinks are visible at first.
  • The full recipe including ingredients and steps appears only after tapping Make Drink.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving the birthday field empty — Let's Go stays inactive without it.
  • Assuming only one drink category exists; Mocktails was confirmed but other tabs may be available.
  • Not tapping Make Drink — the full recipe is hidden until that step.
