Spinning Void Circles guide

Spinning Void Circles presents a CIRCLES counter over a galaxy background. Our replay opened the game and drew multiple strokes, but the counter stayed at 0, so the guide records the recognition blocker instead of pretending the route cleared.

Play source game
Quick answer

Draw closed, smooth circles inside the play area and watch whether CIRCLES increments. Our replay did not register a circle, so the current verified state is counter 0 with unrecognized strokes.

How to play

  • Open the source link and wait for the CIRCLES counter to appear.
  • Draw a closed loop inside the central play area.
  • Check whether the counter increments after the loop closes.
  • Our replay drew visible strokes, but CIRCLES remained 0.

Controls

  • Drag in a smooth circular path.
  • Close the loop cleanly instead of drawing a short slash.
  • Stay away from the bottom Loopit controls and the top status area.

Strategy tips

  • Use slower circles with a consistent radius.
  • Start and end at nearly the same point to improve recognition.
  • Try one complete circle at a time, then pause to see if the counter updates.
  • Because our replay did not register, a future full guide should include video and frame review.

Common mistakes

  • Drawing a partial arc or slash instead of a closed loop.
  • Drawing too close to the app controls.
  • Assuming visible ink means the circle was accepted by the game.
