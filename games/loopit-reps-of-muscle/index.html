Reps of Muscle guide

Reps of Muscle is a workout clicker with money, Store, Save, Rank, and Reset. The July 13 replay reached REPS: 10 and $20, inspected both store upgrades, opened Rank, used Save, and verified Reset returns the game to zero.

Last updated July 13, 2026

Play source game
Quick answer

Tap the workout canvas to gain 1 rep and $2 per registered input. The first store purchase costs $50, so build at least 25 reps before buying Stronger Lift.

How to play

  • The start state is REPS: 0 and $0.
  • The verified run reached REPS: 10 and $20.
  • Store lists Stronger Lift (+1) for $50 and Auto Lifter (+1/s) for $100.
  • Reset returned the counters to REPS: 0 and $0.

Controls

  • Tap the character/workout canvas to gain reps and money.
  • Use Store to inspect or buy Stronger Lift and Auto Lifter.
  • Use Save after progress, Rank for the global ranking panel, and Reset to clear the run.

Strategy tips

  • Build to at least $50 before trying the first purchase.
  • Save after a meaningful milestone, then verify the visible counters before leaving.
  • Use Reset only after recording your result because it immediately returns progress to zero.

Common mistakes

  • Expecting every automated browser click to register; the canvas can coalesce fast inputs.
  • Trying to buy Stronger Lift below $50.
  • Using Reset before capturing the current reps and money.
