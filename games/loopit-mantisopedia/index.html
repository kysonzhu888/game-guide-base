Mantisopedia guide

Mantisopedia is a clickable mantis encyclopedia with species profiles and unlockable facts. The verified run opened Orchid Mantis and revealed all three of its facts.

Last updated July 16, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Open Orchid Mantis and click UNLOCK NEW FACT three times; the profile reaches 3/3 Facts and reveals all verified Orchid Mantis notes.

How to play

  • The encyclopedia exposed 23 named species buttons in the observed list.
  • Selecting Orchid Mantis opens the Hymenopus coronatus profile.
  • The profile identifies it as harmless, medium-sized at 5-8 cm, and native to Southeast Asian tropical rainforests.
  • Three presses on UNLOCK NEW FACT changed the counter to 3/3 Facts.

Controls

  • Tap a species name to open its profile.
  • Tap UNLOCK NEW FACT once for each hidden fact.
  • Read the fact counter to confirm how many profile facts are unlocked.

Strategy tips

  • Open one species at a time so its facts remain tied to the correct profile.
  • Watch the counter after every unlock instead of assuming a tap registered.
  • Capture the completed 3/3 state when documenting a fully revealed species.

Common mistakes

  • Treating the species list as decoration instead of interactive profile buttons.
  • Stopping before the fact counter reaches 3/3.
  • Generalizing the Orchid Mantis facts to the other 22 listed species.

Mantisopedia FAQ

What is the Orchid Mantis scientific name?

Hymenopus coronatus.

How do I reveal all Orchid Mantis facts?

Open its profile and press UNLOCK NEW FACT three times until the counter reads 3/3 Facts.

Which facts were revealed?

Its camouflage can attract more pollinators than real flowers, nymphs resemble firebugs before developing flower mimicry, and it can adjust color intensity to nearby flowers.
