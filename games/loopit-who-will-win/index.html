Who Will Win guide

Who Will Win is a popular Loopit physics spinner. Web replay loaded the wheel and reduced the Remaining counter from 70 to 67 after drag input.

Last updated July 2, 2026

Play source game
Quick answer

Drag the green wheel in a circular motion and watch the Remaining counter. Our replay verified movement from Remaining 70 to Remaining 67, not a full clear.

How to play

  • The game opens with a green circular wheel full of country-flag balls.
  • The main objective is tracked by a Remaining counter that starts at 70.
  • A drag gesture spins or tilts the wheel; after replay input the counter reached 67.
  • The yellow lightning button is visible, but this replay did not verify its full effect.

Controls

  • Drag along the wheel rim rather than tapping the center.
  • Use short circular swipes to move the pile without losing track of the counter.
  • Pause after each movement and check whether Remaining changed.

Strategy tips

  • Start with a slow clockwise drag so the balls settle together.
  • Aim to create a gap at the open part of the wheel before dragging again.
  • Use the Remaining counter as the only verified progress signal.
  • A full guide update should continue until the counter reaches 0 or a result screen appears.

Common mistakes

  • Tapping instead of dragging the wheel.
  • Assuming the level is solved while Remaining is still above 0.
  • Dragging too aggressively and making the ball pile harder to read.
