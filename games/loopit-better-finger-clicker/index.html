Better Finger Clicker guide

This Loopit playable is a classic clicker. Tap the large blue button to gain clicks, then buy Better Finger for stronger manual taps before saving for Auto Tapper.

Play source game
Quick answer

Tap the big blue TAP button until you can buy Better Finger for 10 clicks. Buy that first, then keep tapping toward Auto Tapper and Goal 1.

How to play

  • Start on Goal 1 with 0 out of 100,000 progress.
  • Each tap on the large blue TAP button adds 1 click at the beginning.
  • The first visible upgrade is Better Finger, which costs 10 clicks at level 0.
  • Auto Tapper starts locked behind a higher cost, so do not wait for it before improving manual clicks.
  • After reaching 10 clicks in our recording, Better Finger became the first practical upgrade.
  • Continue alternating between tapping and upgrades to move toward Goal 1.

Controls

  • Tap the large blue TAP button repeatedly.
  • Tap the Better Finger upgrade card when you have at least 10 clicks.
  • Watch the per-click and per-second labels under the progress number.
  • Do not tap the top upgrade cards too early if you have not earned enough clicks.
  • Use short bursts of tapping so the UI has time to update after buying an upgrade.

Strategy tips

  • Buy Better Finger first because it improves the active tapping route immediately.
  • Delay Auto Tapper until manual clicking has produced enough surplus.
  • Use the top cards as your upgrade checklist: Better Finger first, Auto Tapper later, locked cards after that.
  • If the cost number is red or does not respond, return to the TAP button and earn more clicks.
  • Goal 1 is long, so the game is about upgrade pacing rather than a short puzzle solution.

Common mistakes

  • Trying to buy Auto Tapper before funding Better Finger.
  • Tapping the top cards instead of the large button at the start.
  • Missing the 10-click threshold for the first upgrade.
  • Assuming the goal can be reached without upgrades.
  • Stopping after a few taps because the 100,000 target looks too large.

Better Finger Clicker FAQ

What should I buy first in Better Finger Clicker?

Buy Better Finger first once you reach 10 clicks. It is the earliest useful upgrade in our run.

How do you earn clicks?

Tap the large blue TAP button. The starting value is 1 click per tap.

When should I buy Auto Tapper?

After Better Finger. Auto Tapper costs more, so saving for it before the first manual upgrade slows the start.

What is Goal 1?

Goal 1 is the first long progress target, shown as 0 out of 100,000 at the start.

Is this a reflex game?

No. It is an incremental clicker. Upgrade order matters more than timing precision.
