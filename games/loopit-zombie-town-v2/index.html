Zombie Town V2 guide

Zombie Town V2 has clear counters and placement tools. Our replay moved PEOPLE from 0 to 9 while zombies stayed 0.

Play source game
Quick answer

Start by placing people and houses before adding zombies or hazards. Our replay verified PEOPLE rising from 0 to 9.

How to play

  • Counters track PEOPLE, ZOMBIES, and HOUSES.
  • Tool buttons include house, person, zombie, and hazard icons.
  • Our replay placed people and moved the people counter from 0 to 9.
  • No full outbreak ending was captured.

Controls

  • Tap a tool icon, then tap the map.
  • Use people and houses before adding zombies.
  • Use hazard tools after the basic town is visible.

Strategy tips

  • Build a readable layout first.
  • Add zombies slowly so you can observe spread.
  • Track PEOPLE and ZOMBIES counters after each tap.
  • Reset only after recording the state you want to compare.

Common mistakes

  • Adding hazards before placing people.
  • Ignoring counters.
  • Overfilling the map until changes are unreadable.
