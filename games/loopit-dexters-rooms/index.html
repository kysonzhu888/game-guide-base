Dexter's Rooms guide

Dexter's Rooms is a Loopit room exploration game by DECsta. Browser gameplay on July 25, 2026 confirmed: a loaded game screen, a Swing Room environment with interactive objects, an items-found state, and navigation to the next room.

Last updated July 25, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Open the game, enter the first room and interact with objects to find hidden items. Use the navigation controls to move to the next room and continue exploring.

How to play

  • The game loads directly into a room environment without a separate title screen.
  • Each room contains interactive objects and hidden items to discover.
  • The Swing Room was the first confirmed environment.
  • Finding items in a room unlocks navigation to the next room.
  • Multiple rooms are connected in sequence.

Controls

  • Tap or click on objects in the room to interact with them.
  • Explore the full room area to find all hidden items.
  • Use the next-room button or arrow to advance when all items are found.
  • Review your found items list to track progress.

Strategy tips

  • Tap every visible object in a room — items can be hidden behind ordinary-looking furniture or decorations.
  • Check corners and edges of the room view for off-center items.
  • The items-found indicator shows how many items remain before you can advance.
  • Move to the next room only after you have collected everything in the current one to avoid missing items.

Common mistakes

  • Advancing to the next room before finding all items in the current room.
  • Tapping only the center of the room and missing objects near the edges.
  • Overlooking the items list indicator that shows remaining undiscovered items.
