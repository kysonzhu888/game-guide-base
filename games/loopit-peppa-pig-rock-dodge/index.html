Peppa Pig guide

Peppa Pig is a Loopit obstacle runner. Our run verified the Score 0 start state, a later Score 3 live state after a drag or jump, and a 12-frame browser capture.

Last updated July 20, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Tap or click to jump, then keep the runner clear of rocks. The verified route advances from Score 0 to Score 3 during live play.

How to play

  • The start state shows Score 0 and a Tap to Jump hint.
  • A live drag or jump changed the visible score from 0 to 3.
  • The page keeps the score visible while the runner is active.

Controls

  • Tap or click to jump.
  • Use the game surface to keep the runner clear of rocks.
  • Watch the score as a live progress signal.

Strategy tips

  • Wait for the first obstacle before judging the pace.
  • Use the score change as confirmation that the runner is still live.
  • Keep your jumps simple and timed rather than spamming the control.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming the start state is static.
  • Spamming random clicks instead of timing the jump.
  • Claiming a win path when the verified route only proved a score increase.
