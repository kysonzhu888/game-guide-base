Red vs Blue Warriors guide

Red vs Blue Warriors exposes a unit roster for a battle sandbox. Our replay verified the available unit buttons and split battlefield.

Last updated July 2, 2026

Play source game
Quick answer

Start with Warriors, add Archers behind them, then use Healer or Trapper to support. Boss and Freeze should come after the board has units.

How to play

  • Visible unit buttons include Warrior, Archer, Juggernaut, Healer, Trapper, and Boss.
  • Reset and Freeze are also available.
  • The board is split red versus blue.
  • Our replay verified the roster, not a full battle outcome.

Controls

  • Tap a unit button to select it.
  • Place units on the battlefield.
  • Use Reset to clear and Freeze to pause or control action if needed.

Strategy tips

  • Build a front line with Warriors first.
  • Place Archers behind tougher units.
  • Use Healers after you have units worth supporting.
  • Do not place Boss too early if you want to observe normal unit behavior.

Common mistakes

  • Dropping advanced units before learning basic Warrior behavior.
  • Forgetting Reset exists.
  • Overcrowding one side before testing balance.
