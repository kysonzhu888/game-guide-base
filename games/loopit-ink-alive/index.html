Ink Alive guide

Ink Alive is a Loopit drawing playable. Web replay showed the title card, the instruction Draw it. Click it. Watch it Move!, and a large Play button.

Last updated July 8, 2026 · 1 tracked update

Play source game
Quick answer

Press Play, then follow the draw-click-watch loop. This pass verified the start card and instructions, but not the full drawing canvas.

How to play

  • The title card reads INK ALIVE!
  • The instruction says Draw it. Click it. Watch it Move!
  • A large Play button starts the route.
  • The game appears to be about animating or moving drawn ink.

Controls

  • Tap Play to enter the drawing route.
  • Draw only after the canvas is visible.
  • Click or tap the drawn object to test movement.

Strategy tips

  • Read the three-step instruction before pressing Play.
  • Make a simple drawing first so movement is easy to see.
  • Capture before and after movement if you are documenting a successful run.

Common mistakes

  • Expecting movement before pressing Play.
  • Drawing something too complex for the first test.
  • Calling the guide complete before verifying the canvas.
