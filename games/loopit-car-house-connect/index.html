Car House Connect guide

Car House Connect opens on a Level 1 grid with red and blue cars and matching houses. Our replay verified the puzzle objective and board layout.

Play source game
Quick answer

Connect each car to the house of the same color without blocking the other color path. Start with the longest or most constrained route first.

How to play

  • Level 1 says to connect cars to matching houses.
  • The board uses separate colored endpoints.
  • Draw routes that do not trap the second car.
  • Our replay verified Level 1 but did not capture a clear overlay.

Controls

  • Drag from a car toward its matching house.
  • Use short route corrections around corners.
  • Restart if a route blocks the other color.

Strategy tips

  • Plan both colors before drawing.
  • Route the longer path first if one color has fewer options.
  • Keep the center cells open until the second route is clear.
  • Avoid crossing paths unless the game explicitly allows it.

Common mistakes

  • Connecting to the wrong color house.
  • Filling the middle too early.
  • Assuming one solved car means the level is done.
