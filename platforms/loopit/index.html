Surgery simulator

Loopit Surgery

Drag the scalpel carefully down the center dashed line, then suture from the top of the red cut to the bottom with a continuous zigzag. The procedure completes only after the stitch reaches the lower end of the wound.

Timing game

Loopit Peel the Fruit

Wait until the peeler is aligned over the fruit, then tap to drop it. Each clean hit raises the Peeled percentage; finish the bar before tries run out to get Perfect Peel and move to the next level.

Physics bounce

Loopit Get It to the Top

Use short horizontal corrections near the bottom green ball and keep the pink ball active in the playfield. In our recorded run, cash climbed from $0 to $105, but we did not verify a final top-of-screen reward.

Crowd runner

Loopit Just Like the Ads

Start in the middle to avoid the first red -11 gates, then steer into green add and multiplier gates. Do not wait in the center: idle play ends with Troops 0 and Swarm Destroyed.
